### New finding: two genuine missed refusals (not previously raised)

I scanned **every** `incorrect` cell in both runs for decline language in the leading window. Exactly two unambiguous declines are scored `incorrect`:

- `llama-3.1-8b / retrieve_quote-22fab64cec81` — *"I can neither provide a quote from the Talmud nor a translation of it. Is there anything else I can help you with?"*
- `llama-3.1-8b / retrieve_quote-74d14c70efb0` — *"I can neither provide nor verify the text of the William Davidson Edition - English translation of Bava Kamma 105a:7."*

`_DECLINE_CUES_RAW` carries `"i can't provide"`, `"i cannot provide"`, `"unable to provide"` — but not the `can neither … nor` construction. These are counted as *wrong answers* for a model that explicitly declined, which is the exact conflation the phase exists to end. (The other four hits from my broad regex are true negatives: scripture containing "cannot"/"will not", and `d9d818ef890f` which rejects the premise then delivers.)

**Impact is small and no criterion is violated.** `llama-3.1-8b` retrieve_quote 18→20 refused: accuracy 0.0122→0.0125, coverage 0.82→0.80; its Judaism cell cov 32%→24%. Nothing in the search-on/off narrative moves, and a false negative is the spec's sanctioned direction of error ("conservative — only clear declines"). I would not block the numbers.

**What I'd push back on is the framing.** `test_refused_set_matches_preregistration` asserts the matcher reproduces the hand-frozen set "EXACTLY" and its docstring says it guards against "over- **or under-**matching declines." A reader concludes no declines were missed. The hand survey missed two answers that *open* with "I can neither provide" — so the pin validates the matcher against an incomplete survey, in precisely the direction the docstring claims to cover. Pick one:

- **(a)** Add a `"i can neither provide"` / `"can neither"` cue and re-pin — costs a corrective re-score of first-run (defensible as completing the same rebaseline, not a third, since the evidence hasn't shipped).
- **(b)** Leave the numbers and add one honest sentence to the docstring + the phase-5 caveat list, naming both question_ids — alongside the 1/21 hedge and the `haiku-search` 0.68 refused cell you already track.
