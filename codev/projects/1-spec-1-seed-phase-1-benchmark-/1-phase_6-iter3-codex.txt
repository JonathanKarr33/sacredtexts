SUMMARY: Core scoring and raw-count aggregation work, but failed cells disappear from the tradition breakdown and lowercase citation hedges bypass the anti-hedge rule.
CONFIDENCE: HIGH
---
KEY_ISSUES:
- The expected roster is keyed only by `(model_id, task)`. `by_tradition` is built exclusively from successful answer rows, so if a tradition’s cell fails entirely, that model × task × tradition cell is dropped rather than represented. Pass tradition-aware expected counts derived from the questions and test a failed tradition cell.
- Citation extraction is case-sensitive. For expected `John 1:1`, `"it could be john 1:1 or genesis 1:1"` returns `correct=1`; lowercase hadith hedges can likewise pass with zero candidates. This violates the required multi-candidate-hedge rejection. Make extraction case-insensitive and add lowercase hedge tests.
